# Death Music

Changes the music every time you die.

When you die, the mod picks another NONG that you already saved in Jukebox for the song of the level you are playing, and plays it after the restart. It never repeats the same NONG twice in a row. Nothing gets downloaded, it only uses what you already have.

## Settings

- **Change music on death**: turns the switching on or off.
- **Change from level percent**: the NONG only changes if you die at this percent or later. Default is 60.

## Good to know

- Needs the Jukebox mod.
- The level's song needs at least two saved NONGs, otherwise the music stays the same.
- Works with songs from Newgrounds. Official songs are not supported.

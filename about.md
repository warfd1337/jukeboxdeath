# Death Music

Changes the music every time you die.

When you die, the mod picks another NONG that you already saved in Jukebox for the song of the level you are playing, and plays it after the restart. It never repeats the same NONG twice in a row. Nothing gets downloaded, it only uses what you already have.

## Pause menu

Two buttons show up in the bottom corner of the pause menu:

- **Music note**: opens the settings of this mod.
- **Jukebox disc**: opens the NONG pool of the current song. Check the NONGs you want in the rotation and uncheck the ones you don't. "Shuffle now" picks another NONG for the next restart.

## Settings

- **Change music on death**: turns the switching on or off.
- **Change from level percent**: the NONG only changes if you die at this percent or later. Default is 60.
- **Use random chance**: ignores the percent. Every death has a chance to change the NONG instead.
- **Change chance (%)**: the chance used by the option above. Default is 50.
- **Change music in start pos**: off by default, so playing from a start pos keeps the same music.
- **Pause button on the left**: moves the pause menu buttons between the bottom left and bottom right corner.

## Good to know

- Needs the Jukebox mod.
- The level's song needs at least two saved NONGs in the pool, otherwise the music stays the same.
- Works with songs from Newgrounds and with official RobTop songs.
